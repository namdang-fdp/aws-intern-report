This comprehensive dossier encapsulates the entire operational trajectory executed throughout the **First Cloud Journey (FCJ)** internship program at **AWS**. It serves as a definitive chronicle of the learning curve, architectural implementation of the **Bandup IELTS** project, complex troubleshooting resolutions, and engagement in specialized industry events over a **12-week intensive period** (approx. 3 months).

Spanning these 12 weeks, the journey evolved from grasping foundational Cloud paradigms to engineering, optimizing, and deploying a production-grade **AI-Powered Serverless Application** on AWS. This milestone is underpinned by the successful completion of **50+ AWS Skill Builder** accreditations.

### Developmental Trajectory

**Weeks 1-5: Foundation & Infrastructure**

-   **Core Proficiency:** Mastered the foundational pillars (EC2, S3, VPC, IAM).
-   **Network Engineering:** Designed complex topologies using Route 53 and Transit Gateway.
-   **Strategic Planning:** Focused on Cost Optimization and High-Availability Architecture.
-   **IaC Adoption:** Shifted towards Infrastructure as Code via CloudFormation.

**Weeks 6-7: Consolidation & Assessment**

-   **Storage Mastery:** Deepened expertise in storage hierarchies and lifecycle policies.
-   **Resilience:** Formulated Disaster Recovery and Business Continuity strategies.
-   **Validation:** Synthesized knowledge for the comprehensive Mid-term Assessment.

**Weeks 8-10: Implementation & Integration**

-   **Serverless Shift:** Transitioned to Event-Driven Architectures (Lambda, DynamoDB).
-   **Framework Adoption:** Standardized development on **AWS SAM** and **Docker**.
-   **Stabilization:** Overcame interoperability challenges (CORS, Auth) to achieve Full-Stack Integration.

**Weeks 11-12: Innovation & Optimization**

-   **Architectural Refinement:** Decoupled monolithic stacks into optimized **Multi-Stack** formations.
-   **AI-Infusion:** Integrated Generative AI capabilities (Bedrock, Gemini) and RAG pipelines.
-   **Project Completion:** Delivered a production-ready, fully featured Bandup IELTS platform.
