> This section shares my personal experience and feedback from participating in the First Cloud Journey (FCJ) program at AWS, hoping to help the FCJ team continue improving the program for future interns.

---

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**
The working environment at AWS exceeded my expectations. The office is modern, well-equipped, and designed for productivity. What impressed me most was the **open culture** where everyone, regardless of seniority, is approachable and willing to help. The remote/hybrid work flexibility was also greatly appreciated, allowing me to balance learning and development effectively.

The FCJ team created a supportive atmosphere where asking questions was encouraged, not judged. Having access to AWS resources and services for hands-on learning was invaluable - I could experiment with real cloud infrastructure instead of just reading documentation.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**
The mentorship I received was **exceptional**. My mentor didn't just provide answers - they guided me through the problem-solving process, helping me develop critical thinking skills for cloud architecture decisions. Key highlights:

-   **Weekly 1:1 sessions** to review progress and address architectural challenges.
-   **Code reviews** (PRs) that significantly improved my Java Spring Boot coding standards and frontend optimization.
-   **Architecture discussions** that helped me understand the "why" behind choosing ECS Fargate over other compute options.
-   **Career guidance** on AWS certifications and Full-stack engineering paths.

The admin team efficiently handled all logistics - from account access to resource provisioning - so I could focus entirely on learning and building.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**
The BandUp IELTS project was **perfectly aligned** with my computer science background while pushing me into new territories of Cloud Native development:

| Academic Knowledge Applied | New Skills Learned                            |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| OOP & Java Programming     | Spring Boot Enterprise Architecture           |
| Web Development (React)    | Next.js, Bun & Client-side Optimization       |
| Database Design            | Amazon RDS (PostgreSQL) & ElastiCache (Redis) |
| Networking & OS            | Docker, AWS ECS Fargate, VPC Networking       |
| Software Engineering       | CI/CD Pipelines, Infrastructure as Code       |

The project challenged me to integrate AI services (Gemini API, Bedrock) into a production-ready system - something far beyond classroom exercises.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**
The FCJ program provided **exceptional learning opportunities**:

-   **Hands-on AWS experience** with core services (ECS, Fargate, ALB, RDS, ElastiCache, S3, CloudFront).
-   **Real project ownership** - I wasn't just doing small tasks; I built the full-stack flow from Frontend to Backend.
-   **Cost optimization skills** - Learned to implement cost-effective AI strategies (saving ~72% on audio processing).
-   **Documentation practices** - Created this comprehensive workshop guide as a knowledge-sharing artifact.
-   **Exposure to AI/ML** - Integrated cutting-edge AI models for practical education applications.

The self-paced learning combined with mentor guidance struck the perfect balance for skill development.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**
AWS's culture of **customer obsession** and **ownership** was evident throughout my internship:

-   Team members genuinely cared about helping me succeed.
-   Failures were treated as learning opportunities, not criticism.
-   Innovation was encouraged - my suggestions for the Gemini native audio approach were welcomed and implemented.
-   The "Day 1" mentality kept everyone motivated and forward-thinking.

I felt like a valued contributor, not just "the intern." My work mattered, and that made all the difference in my engagement and motivation.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**
The FCJ program offered excellent support:

-   ✅ Competitive internship stipend.
-   ✅ Flexible working hours accommodating student schedules.
-   ✅ Access to AWS training resources and certifications.
-   ✅ Internal tech talks and learning sessions.
-   ✅ Networking opportunities with AWS professionals.
-   ✅ Real project experience for portfolio building.

### Reflection Questions

**What did I find most satisfying during my internship?**

Building a system that truly works. Seeing the BandUp platform evaluating IELTS essays and handling user sessions seamlessly across ECS containers gave me immense satisfaction. The moment the connection between Next.js frontend, Spring Boot backend, and the AI services stabilized on the AWS cloud environment was a highlight I'll never forget.

**What could be improved for future interns?**

-   **Earlier AWS account access:** Having full access from day 1 would accelerate the learning curve.
-   **More structured onboarding:** A checklist of "must-know" AWS concepts (specifically IAM and VPC) would help new interns ramp up faster.
-   **Peer intern collaboration:** Pairing interns on complementary projects (e.g., Dev vs. Ops) could enhance learning.
-   **More exposure to other teams:** Shadowing opportunities with different AWS teams would broaden perspective.

**Would I recommend this internship to friends?**

**Absolutely yes.** For anyone interested in cloud computing and modern software development, the FCJ program at AWS is unmatched. You get:

-   Hands-on experience with industry-leading cloud services.
-   Mentorship from experienced professionals.
-   Real project ownership and responsibility.
-   Skills that are highly valued in the job market.

The only prerequisite is a genuine willingness to learn and work hard.

---

### Suggestions & Future Expectations

**Suggestions to improve the internship experience:**

1. **Create an intern resource hub:** Consolidated documentation, tutorials, and FAQs for common challenges (especially regarding networking and permissions).
2. **Bi-weekly demo sessions:** Interns present their work to the broader team for feedback and visibility.
3. **AWS certification support:** Vouchers or study groups for Cloud Practitioner/Solutions Architect exams.
4. **Alumni network:** Connect current interns with FCJ alumni for career advice.

**Would I like to continue with this program?**

Yes! I would love to:

-   Return as a full-time Cloud/Software Engineer after graduation.
-   Mentor future FCJ interns, paying forward the guidance I received.
-   Continue contributing to FCJ workshops and documentation.

**Final thoughts:**

This internship transformed my understanding of cloud computing and full-stack integration. I arrived knowing basic programming; I leave with the ability to design and implement scalable containerized architectures. The FCJ team didn't just teach me AWS - they showed me what it means to be a professional engineer.

Thank you, AWS and the FCJ team, for this incredible opportunity. 🙏
